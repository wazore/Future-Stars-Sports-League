Refund Policy

Future Stars Sports League is committed to providing a positive and enjoyable sports experience for all participants.

Registration Fees: Players, Uniforms, and Flags

Registration fees may cover player participation, uniforms, flags, and related program costs. Where costs have already been incurred on a participant’s behalf, those costs may be deducted from an approved refund, as permitted by applicable law.

Refund Requests

Refund requests must be submitted in writing before the start of the applicable season or program. Approved refunds may be subject to deductions for non-refundable administrative fees, uniforms, equipment, or other costs already incurred on behalf of the participant.

After the Season Begins

Once a season or program has started, registration fees are generally non-refundable, except in cases approved by league management or where a refund is required by applicable law.

Cancellations by the League

If Future Stars Sports League cancels a season or program and cannot offer a suitable alternative, registered participants will be eligible for a refund or other resolution determined by the league, subject to applicable law.

Weather and Schedule Changes

Games, practices, or events postponed or rescheduled due to weather, facility availability, or other circumstances beyond the league’s control do not automatically qualify for a refund. Any applicable make-up arrangements or other options will be communicated by the league.

How to Request a Refund

All refund requests must be submitted to Future Stars Sports League through the league’s official contact information. Requests will be reviewed individually, and the league will communicate its decision to the requesting family.

Acceptance of This Policy

By completing registration and submitting payment, participants and their parents or guardians acknowledge that they have read this refund policy.

Please review this policy before registering. This page provides general program information and does not limit any rights families may have under applicable law.
