Refund Policy

Future Stars Sports League is committed to providing a positive, safe, and enjoyable sports experience for all participants and their families. Please review this policy carefully before completing registration and submitting payment.

1. Registration Fees, Uniforms, and Flags

Registration fees may cover player participation, uniforms, flags, equipment, administrative expenses, and other costs associated with operating the program.

When a registration is completed, certain expenses may be incurred on behalf of the participant. These may include ordering uniforms, purchasing flags or equipment, processing registration, and other program-related expenses.

Any deductions from an approved refund for administrative fees, uniforms, flags, equipment, or other costs already incurred on behalf of a participant will be communicated as applicable and will be subject to applicable law.

2. Refund Requests Before the Season Begins

Refund requests must be submitted in writing before the start of the applicable season or program.

Requests will be reviewed individually by league management. Approved refunds may be subject to deductions for non-refundable administrative fees, uniforms, flags, equipment, or other costs already incurred on behalf of the participant.

Submitting a refund request does not automatically guarantee approval of a full refund.

3. Refunds After the Season Begins

Once a season, program, or scheduled activity has started, registration fees are generally non-refundable.

Exceptions may be considered at the discretion of Future Stars Sports League management, taking into account the circumstances of the request and any applicable legal requirements.

Families are encouraged to contact the league as soon as possible if an unexpected circumstance affects their child's participation.

4. Cancellations by Future Stars Sports League

If Future Stars Sports League cancels a season or program and cannot offer a suitable alternative, registered participants will be eligible for a refund or another appropriate resolution determined by the league, subject to applicable law.

If an alternative program, replacement date, or other arrangement is offered, the league will communicate the available options to registered families.

5. Weather, Rescheduling, and Facility Changes

Games, practices, and events may be postponed, rescheduled, relocated, or modified because of severe weather, unsafe playing conditions, facility availability, or other circumstances beyond the league's reasonable control.

A postponement, rescheduling, relocation, or change to the program schedule does not automatically qualify a participant for a refund.

Future Stars Sports League will communicate relevant schedule changes and any available make-up arrangements to affected families.

6. Player Withdrawal or Failure to Participate

Parents or guardians who decide to withdraw their child from a program must submit their request in writing to Future Stars Sports League.

Refund eligibility will depend on when the request is received, whether the season has begun, and whether program-related costs have already been incurred on the participant's behalf.

Failure to attend practices, games, or other scheduled activities does not automatically qualify a participant for a refund.

7. How to Request a Refund

All refund requests must be submitted in writing through the league's official contact information.

Please include the following information:

  • Parent or guardian's name
  • Participant's name
  • Program or season for which the participant registered
  • Date of registration or payment, if available
  • Reason for the refund request

8. Refund Processing

If a refund is approved, the league will communicate the approved amount and any applicable deductions to the requesting family.

Approved refunds will generally be returned through the original payment method when possible. Processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and financial institution.

9. Policy Compliance and Applicable Law

This policy is intended to explain the league's general refund practices. Nothing in this policy is intended to waive or limit any rights or protections that participants or their families may have under applicable law.

Future Stars Sports League reserves the right to review and update this policy when necessary. Any updated policy will be made available to families before it applies to new registrations.

10. Acknowledgment of the Refund Policy

By completing registration and submitting payment, parents or legal guardians acknowledge that they have had an opportunity to review this Refund Policy and understand the league's general refund procedures.

Families are encouraged to contact Future Stars Sports League with any questions about this policy before completing registration.
