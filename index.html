We plan to use PlayMetrics for registration and payments when enrollment opens. We’ll share the official registration link when it is ready. Please do not send payment before official registration opens.

Absolutely. Contact us by email or social media to discuss family interest, volunteering, community partnerships, or sponsorship opportunities.
